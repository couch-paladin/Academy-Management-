We stand behind the quality of our programmes. If you're not satisfied, we offer a straightforward refund process.

All paid courses and programmes come with a 7-day money-back guarantee from the date of enrolment. If you are not satisfied for any reason, contact us within 7 days and we will process a full refund — no questions asked.

2. Eligibility for Refund

To be eligible for a refund, the following conditions must be met:

  • Refund request submitted within 7 days of enrolment
  • Less than 30% of the course content has been accessed
  • No certificate has been issued
  • The request is made by the original registered student

3. Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refund:

  • Workshop and seminar registrations after the event date has passed
  • 1-on-1 coaching sessions that have already been conducted
  • Courses where more than 30% of content has been accessed
  • Study materials and downloadable resources once accessed
  • Free programmes (no payment, no refund applicable)

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  1. Email us at hello@eduvance.com with the subject "Refund Request"
  2. Include your registered email, programme name, and reason for refund
  3. Our team will review and respond within 2 business days
  4. Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method

5. Partial Refunds

In exceptional circumstances, we may offer partial refunds at our discretion — for example, if a programme is cancelled or significantly changed after enrolment. We will always communicate proactively if this occurs.

6. Programme Cancellations by EduVance

If EduVance cancels a programme before it begins, all enrolled students will receive a full refund within 5 business days. If a programme is rescheduled, students may choose to attend the new date or receive a full refund.
