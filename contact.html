Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course? ▾
Just head to the Courses page, pick a course, and click Enroll. You'll be guided through registration and payment in a couple of minutes.
Are the certificates recognized? ▾
Yes — all our certificates are industry-recognized and can be shared directly on your LinkedIn profile and resume.
Do you offer placement guarantees? ▾
We offer end-to-end placement support with a 94% placement rate. While we can't legally "guarantee" a job, our Elite coaching plan supports you until you're placed.
Can I get a refund? ▾
Yes, we offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all paid courses if you're not satisfied. Reach out to our support team to process it.
Are there free courses available? ▾
Absolutely. We offer several free self-paced courses and free webinars. Look for the "Free" badge on the Courses page.
